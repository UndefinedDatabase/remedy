## Goal & Done
No decision without its receipts: every decision producer MUST
attach the evidence triple — evidence_refs[] (clickable into the
panel), expected_outcome, downside — the inbox card renders all
three, and producers without them fail their own tests. DONE when
the decision schema enforces the triple (with a migration story for
legacy entries), every existing producer is upgraded with
meaningful triples (not boilerplate — reviewed per producer), the
card's evidence chips deep-link into the evidence panel, and a
canary producer missing a field fails CI.

## How it fits (inspect current shape before building)
This is a CONTRACT-RAISING feature: the decision record grows three
required fields (schema-versioned — decision_v2; legacy v1 entries
render with an honest "recorded before evidence requirements"
placeholder rather than fake triples); the enforcement point is the
enqueue seam every producer already funnels through (one gate);
refs use the typed provenance vocabulary (file/failure/decision
kinds cover the current producers; the resolver's badges render on
the chips). The per-producer upgrade is the real work: each
producer states ITS truth — a budget stop's expected_outcome is
the extend/abandon consequence math; a veto proposal's downside is
the reduced scope; boilerplate triples are the failure mode the
tests must catch (goldens per producer with content assertions,
not presence checks).

## Design (suggested shape)
- Schema: decision_v2 {evidence_refs: min 1 typed ref,
  expected_outcome: str per option (keyed to the options list —
  outcomes are per-choice, that's the point), downside: str per
  option}; enqueue validates; producers construct.
- Producer upgrades (one small order each): plan approval (refs:
  plan.md + assumptions; outcomes per approve/reject), budget stop
  (the arithmetic as refs into the budget evidence), escalation
  (the task's question evidence), watchdog trips (the trip
  numbers), veto replan (the veto reason + unreachable set),
  clarifications (each question's impact IS its triple — map,
  don't duplicate), injection shortfall (the three-option math).
- Card rendering: chips with resolver badges → evidence panel deep
  links (the zoom feature's L3 tabs); outcomes/downsides render
  under each option button — consent reads before it clicks.
- CI canary: a test-only producer omitting a field must fail the
  enqueue gate (the guard that keeps future producers honest).

## Task slicing
- **T001** schema v2 + enqueue gate + legacy rendering + the canary
  + unit tests.
- **T002** producer upgrades (per-producer orders with content
  goldens — the anti-boilerplate assertions).
- **T003** card enrichment + chip deep links + the end-to-end (a
  budget-stop card shows the arithmetic refs, clicking opens the
  evidence, the outcome text matches the chosen option's real
  consequence).

## Acceptance
- Gate refuses tripleless enqueues; canary red. Per-producer
  goldens contain producer-specific content (grep against
  boilerplate phrases — a denylist of generic filler). Legacy
  entries render the honest placeholder. Chips resolve and
  deep-link.

## Edge cases & assumption defaults (A9)
- Producers whose downside is genuinely "none material": the
  literal string "no material downside identified" is allowed and
  rendered as such — honesty includes benign cases (but the golden
  reviews it per producer). Refs into pruned evidence: resolver
  staleness badges carry the truth.

## Orchestrator brief
T001 first; T002 is parallel per-producer orders — each order
quotes ITS producer's expected triple content so workers write
truths, not templates. The anti-boilerplate denylist is acceptance
material.

## Do not touch
Decision answering flow, queue storage, provenance resolver.
