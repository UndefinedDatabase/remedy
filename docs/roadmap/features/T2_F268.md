## Goal & Done
DECISION amend0905-vocab D8, verbatim:

`remedy do` is THE easy start and the operator's standing test path. In order:
`init` if the repo is not registered; `study` (F266) exactly once if the repo is
non-empty and never studied (afterwards only by hand); planner produces mission
plan + contract + job plans; shape decision (one job or several); runs start; the
cockpit opens in the browser (unless `--no-ui`); it stops before applying unless
`--apply`. `--step-by-step` halts at every safe point, prints what just happened
and what comes next, waits for Enter (`q` stops). `--plan-only` stops after
planning. `do` never runs a model call while waiting for a keypress.

DONE when the Acceptance list holds on the fake provider.

## Why this exists
Today `do` has three internal modes, measured 2026-09-05 in
`apps/cli/commands/do_cmd.py`: a truly bare `remedy do "<mission>"` takes the F147
golden path (`_cmd_do_mission`: a classic-store job with skeleton or LLM-planned
tasks); `--builder` / `--reviewer` set to a real provider takes `_cmd_do_pingpong`
(a single run without a job); everything else is the autorun path. The `init` hint
(`apps/cli/commands/init_cmd.py`, lines 188 and 211: `Next: remedy do "<your first
mission>"`) points at a command shape that then does something the user did not
ask for. The operator saw all three in tests.md Level 4. One command that does one
sequence, honestly, is the product's usability promise (F147) made true on the
unified model (F260) with the decided words (F259, F261).

## Design
- `do` is a THIN sequence over `init`, `study`, mission planning (F260's records),
  `job run`, `ui start` and `job apply`; it owns no logic of its own except the
  shape decision and the step-by-step halt.
- The shape decision is the planner's: a structured output `"one job"` /
  `"milestones"` from the mission planner (F069's compiler on the F260 record);
  `--force-job` / `--force-mission` override it. The planner's task count is free
  within the `planning.granularity.*` ceilings
  (`packages/orchestration/config.py`: `enabled`, `split_band`, `max_acceptance`,
  `merge_group_size` at `b2ee0a84`) — ceilings, not targets (D1, Task).
- Study-once is recorded on the project record as `studied_at` and
  `studied_head` (no such field exists today; F266 adds the write, `do` reads it).
- `--step-by-step` reuses the safe points where budget and stop are checked today
  (`packages/orchestration/safe_points.py`); at each halt it prints what just
  happened and what comes next and waits for Enter; `q` stops through the F011 kill
  switch. No model call is in flight while waiting.
- `--plan-only` stops after planning and prints the mission plan, the contract and
  the job plans.
- `--apply` passes through to `job apply --approve`; `--with-history` passes
  through to F270's merge. F270 must be `[x]`; if it is not when this feature
  lands, `--with-history` prints "not yet available" and this is a documented
  dependency, not a stub.
- `--json` carries at least `mission_id`, `job_ids` (ordered list), `contract`
  (criteria with status), `stopped_before_apply` (bool) — the operator's acceptance
  script reads exactly these keys.
- Flags, complete (D4): `--apply`, `--with-history`, `--step-by-step`,
  `--plan-only`, `--force-job`, `--force-mission`, `--contract <template>`,
  `--no-ui`, `--project`, `--repo`, the budget flags, the role flags
  (`--builder-model`, `--reviewer-model`, `--planner-model` and the
  `--*-provider` triplet), `--yes`, `--json`. Nothing else under `do`.

## Acceptance
- `remedy do "Write a CONTRIBUTING.md" --no-ui` on an unregistered fixture repo
  with the fake provider registers the repo, studies it, plans ONE job, runs, and
  stops before apply; `git status` in the target is unchanged until `--apply`.
- `--force-mission` on the same order yields ≥ 2 jobs.
- `--step-by-step` halts at least three times; no provider call is made while a
  halt waits (test).
- `--plan-only` writes mission plan, contract and job plans and starts no run.
- `--json` carries `mission_id`, `job_ids`, `contract`, `stopped_before_apply`.
- The quick start's five commands each exit 0 on a fixture repo.
