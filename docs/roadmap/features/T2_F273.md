Pay down the findings that describe a real defect. The 2026-09-06 triage read all
298 open findings and resolved 243 of them — 39 already fixed on main, 204 with no
product effect at all — leaving 55: one deletion-bound finding that F261 discharges

## Why this exists
Because a finding ledger that only grows stops being read. When the amend0827
process diet opened, the record held 270 findings, 253 of them open against 17 ever
resolved, and the diagnosis then was that ids were being spent on the reviewer's own
prose as readily as on the product. That diagnosis was right and the triage measured
it: 204 of 298 — more than two thirds — are the review process describing its own
artifacts, and they buy no repair because there is nothing on disk to repair. What
was left underneath is a real and reviewable list, and it is this one.

The finding text IS the spec. Not one slice below re-plans a fix, states a design or
adds a requirement: each names its ids and its module, and the round that takes it
reads those findings. Where a finding's own text names a fix, that fix binds.
