## Goal & Done
Every blocked, stopped, cancelled or failed job carries a plain readable reason: the story's `stop` with `reason` and `text`, each order job's `stop_text`, and `remedy job show --json` with `stop.text`.

## What it rests on
- Every blocked job of the night of 2026-10-28 had an empty `stop.reason`.

## Acceptance
- A job blocked by its review has a non-empty `stop.text` naming the verdict and its findings' severity.
- The story of that job and its order's job view carry the same sentence.

## Built state (2026-10-29)
Built on branch welt2/remedycore. New `packages/orchestration/job_stop_text.py`: `job_stop(job)` returns `reason` and `text`, the job's own `stop_text` when a step wrote one, else a sentence derived from the task that ended blocked or failed (the review's verdict, its last round's findings by severity read through `job_report.collect_blocked_task_findings`, and the first finding; a failed test run; the run's detail or error), then `stop_reason`, the pause reason and `error`; it is never empty for a blocked, stopped, cancelled or failed job. `client_story.build_client_story` carries it as `stop` (story keys and key tree in `apps/cli/client_interface_story.py`), `order_jobs._job_entry` as `stop_text` (`ORDER_JOBS_KEY_TREE`), `remedy job show --json` as `stop.reason` and `stop.text`; `settle_waiting_jobs` uses it when the awaited job recorded no reason. Interface 1.33 lists them (`docs/system/api-release-matrix-v1.json`). Tests: `tests/orchestration/test_job_stop_text.py`. No new dependency. The story keeps its closed words (exec-1018, R-1347): a task that failed outside its review is named by the story's own reason key (`client_story.story_failure_key`) or its error's leading token, never by the provider's text; only the reviewer's first finding is quoted.

## Do not touch
The non-human content policy; `dangerous-skip`, which stays an explicit opt-in; the human apply gate.
