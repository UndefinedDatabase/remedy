# T4_F144 — Capability ladder
**Tier 4 · Depends on: F103, F082, F074 · Blocks/used by: honest ambition management**

## Goal & Done
Remedy knows — and admits — what size of work it has actually
proven: rungs S/M/L/XL each carry success criteria (pass without
operator intervention, cost and time within the calibrated band),
per-rung history accumulates from real runs, `remedy stats ladder`
shows the proven rung with the evidence, and the planner receives a
HINT when an order exceeds it ("this exceeds the proven rung —
propose a mission of M-sized jobs"), never a refusal. DONE when rung
evaluation matches goldens on fixture histories, the hint appears in
plans/rehearsals above the proven rung and demonstrably shapes the
planner prompt, and nothing anywhere blocks ambition — the ladder
informs, humans and budgets constrain.

## How it fits (inspect current shape before building)
Inputs all exist: task/job bands (plans), terminals and operator-
command data (the autonomy stats' shared definition — import it),
cost/time actuals and calibrated bands (ledger + calibration). A
rung's proof = N successes (config floor) at that band within the
window meeting all criteria; the current proven rung = the highest
proven with all lower rungs proven too (no skipping — a ladder, not
a lottery). The planner hint is a prompt segment addition on the
planning call when the intake's apparent size (band estimate of the
whole mission — the calibration mapping applied to the goal's draft
plan or, pre-plan, the mission compiler's milestone bands) exceeds
the rung.

## Design (suggested shape)
- `ladder.py`: evaluate(project, window) → per rung {attempts,
  successes, criteria breakdown, proven}, current_rung; pure over
  ledger/job queries with the shared operator-command definition;
  small-N honesty like the autonomy stats.
- Criteria per success: green terminal, zero human interventions,
  cost within the rung's calibrated P80, wall within its P80 —
  each criterion's pass/fail recorded so the stats view shows WHY
  a rung isn't proven ("M: 4/5 — one exceeded cost band").
- The hint: planning above the rung injects one segment sentence
  with the rung and the suggestion; the plan renderer surfaces it;
  rehearsals show it prominently (ambition management belongs in
  the dry run). Evidence records ladder_hint: given|not_needed.
- `remedy stats ladder [--json]` + the trend snapshot pattern
  (--record) shared with autonomy stats.

## Task slicing
- **T001** evaluation + criteria breakdown + no-skip rule + goldens
  (proven chains, a broken middle rung, thin history).
- **T002** the hint injection + renderer/rehearsal surfacing +
  evidence + fixtures (below rung: silent; above: the sentence,
  golden).
- **T003** the stats view + why-not breakdown + --record + docs.

## Acceptance
- Fixture goldens exact including the broken-middle case (current
  rung stops below it). The hint appears exactly when it should and
  never blocks (an above-rung fixture still plans and runs). The
  operator-command definition is imported, not redefined (guard).
  Criteria breakdown renders actionably.

## Edge cases & assumption defaults (A9)
- Mixed missions (one L job among S jobs): rung attempts are per
  JOB band; missions inform via their largest job. Regressions (a
  proven rung starts failing): proof is windowed — the rung drops
  out honestly and the stats view shows the recent failures.

## Orchestrator brief
Gate on calibration + autonomy stats. T002's inject-only-above rule
and the never-block rule are verbatim order material. Small feature,
big honesty payoff — protect the tone ("informs, never refuses") in
every rendered string.

## Do not touch
Autonomy levels (orthogonal: who may start vs what size is proven),
budget enforcement, planner internals beyond the segment. Suggested
tests: tests/orchestration/test_capability_ladder.py.
