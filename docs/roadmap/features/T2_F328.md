## Goal & Done
A job that runs unattended and reaches its call or token cap extends once by itself to the next size (the same doubling as the answer `extend`), records the assumption „Remedy hat das Budget einmal selbst erhöht, weil niemand da war“ in its story, and stops at the second touch with the question extend or cancel. The time cap stays hard. An order's body field `auto_extend: false` switches it off (interface 1.34).
