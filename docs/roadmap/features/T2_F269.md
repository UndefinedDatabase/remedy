## Why this exists
The two halves exist — F014's per-task acceptance criteria
(`PlannedTask.acceptance`, `packages/orchestration/schemas/models.py`) and F061's
DoD compiler (`packages/orchestration/dod_compiler.py`, with the
`dod_gate.py` rule "a job ends green only if all blocking checks pass") — but they
have no shared name and no mission-level home. The operator's requirement is that a
mission is not "done" while a blocking criterion is unmet, and that "what a website
must have" can be a reusable template. The overnight Mission Contract prototype
(`packages/orchestration/overnight_mission.py`, `overnight contract-*`) tried the
same idea outside the build path and is deleted by F260 with its heir named here.

## Design
- **Contract record on the mission**: `contract.criteria[]`, each with `text`,
  `blocking`, `check` (compiled by the F061 DoD compiler), `status`,
  `evidence_ref`; plus `contract.template` (name or none) and
  `contract.amendments[]`.
- **Job contract = derived slice**: which criteria this job's tasks serve, computed
  from the mission plan; `mission contract <id>` and `job contract <id>` (D4) render
  the two views.
- **Compiler wiring**: the planner produces criteria from the order; the F061
  compiler turns each into a check; the mission-level gate holds the mission open
  while any blocking criterion is red — the F061 job-end gate generalised one level
  up, reusing `dod_gate.py`, never a second mechanism (Rule A6).
- **Templates** live in `docs/contracts/<name>.md` (human-readable) and compile to
  criteria; the planner proposes one from the order; `--contract <name>` forces
  one. Four ship first: website, api-service, cli-tool, python-library, each with
  one fixture order.
- **Amendments** append to `contract.amendments[]` with the round of effect, are
  re-compiled, and are acknowledged in the run log with what was understood and
  from which round it applies. F264 builds the channel that delivers them; this
  feature owns the data shape and the recompile.
- **Remainder proposal**: at budget end with blocking criteria open, a decision in
  the F031 inbox carrying the open criteria as a prefilled follow-up order; the
  operator answers with one word.
- F072 (Spec-first) is NOT absorbed: the contract is the source its spec renders.

## Acceptance
- A fixture order with a 3-criterion contract where one criterion is unfulfillable
  ends BLOCKED with a remainder decision naming that criterion.
- `--contract website` on a bare order yields the template's criteria.
- An amendment sent between rounds changes the DoD of the next round and is
  acknowledged in the run log with the round it applies from.
- `mission contract <id>` and `job contract <id>` render; the job view is a subset
  of the mission view.
- No second DoD mechanism exists (the compiler and gate are F061's, called).
