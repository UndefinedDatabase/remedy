# T13_F305 — Multi-repo missions: the cockpit's chain band
**Tier 13 · Depends on: F205 · Blocks/used by: the cockpit's view of a mission over several repositories**

## Goal & Done
The cockpit's mission thread shows, for each link of a mission's chain, a chip naming the project
and the repository its job works in, and a click on a chip loads that project's cockpit on that job
(`?project=<project id>&job=<job id>`). Done when a fixture mission over two repositories shows its
chips in a headless browser and a click on a chip of the second project opens that project's
cockpit on that job.

## Acceptance & tests
A fixture mission of three chained jobs across two repositories shows three chips naming two
projects; a click on a chip of the second project opens that project's cockpit on that job; the
chips read references and titles only, never another project's evidence.
R-1248 (Medium, moved here from F297 by DECISION F297 D1) carries a resolution line naming the
browser test of the mission card that finding asks for: one card per repository's mission, its
orders and jobs loaded on demand, with the per-order roll-up the finding names.

## Risks / anti-goals
No global evidence store: the cockpit reads each project's data through that project's own routes.

## Amendment — DECISIONS F305 D1 and D2 (2026-10-12)
This section governs where the text above disagrees with it. Since operator amendment
amend1011b-one-repo-mission a repository has one lasting mission, every order is a batch in it,
and an order over several repositories adds a batch to each repository's own mission, the batches
sharing one correlation id, with no mission over them (DECISIONS amend1011b D1, D3 and D4).

- **The chain.** An order's chain is the jobs of its own batch plus the jobs of every batch, in
  any project's missions, that carries the same correlation id, in the order they were linked. A
  record written before order batches keeps its chain in its links, each naming its project.
- **The card (R-1248).** The cockpit shows one card per mission of the project it shows, newest
  first. Opening a card loads its newest 25 orders, newest first, with a count of the earlier
  ones; each order row names its title, its job count, the calls and tokens its jobs in this
  project made, never a cost in US dollars (DECISION amend1007b D7), and its chain band.
- **The chip** names one job by references and titles only: its id, its project, the folder name
  of its repository, its title and its state. A click opens that project's cockpit on that job.
- **The acceptance fixture** is an order over two repositories whose batches hold three jobs, two
  in the first repository and one in the second: its chain band shows three chips naming two
  projects, from either project's card.
- **The routes** are `/api/projects/<selector>/missions` and
  `/api/projects/<selector>/missions/<mission id>/orders`, answered by
  `packages/orchestration/mission_cockpit.py` through `packages/orchestration/cockpit_project_routes.py`,
  which the cockpit's handler hands every path below a project (DECISION F305 D2).
