# T13_F305 — Multi-repo missions: the cockpit's chain band
**Tier 13 · Depends on: F205 · Blocks/used by: the cockpit's view of a mission over several repositories**

## Goal & Done
The cockpit's mission thread shows, for each link of a mission's chain, a chip naming the project
and the repository its job works in, and a click on a chip loads that project's cockpit on that job
(`?project=<project id>&job=<job id>`). Done when a fixture mission over two repositories shows its
chips in a headless browser and a click on a chip of the second project opens that project's
cockpit on that job.

## Context (existing code & prior features)
Split off F205 at its closure, by DECISION F205 D1 (5) and the split rule of operator amendment
amend0906-split-placement; the parent's closure evidence is named on F205's line in
`docs/roadmap/STATUS.md`. F205 leaves the data this feature reads: a mission's `project_ids` and
each job link's `project_id` in the mission record (DECISION F205 D2), and each job's `repo_path`
and each mission's `project_ids` in the client digest at interface 1.9 (DECISION F205 D6).

## Design
Carried over from F205's file, its Design section: "Cockpit: the mission thread shows repo chips
per chain link; clicking loads that project's cockpit (?project=pid&job=jid)."

## Task slicing
T001, carried from F205's T002: the UI chain band with repo chips. The rest of F205's T002, the
F148 leak regression run over mission data, landed in F205 (DECISION F205 D7).

## Acceptance & tests
A fixture mission of three chained jobs across two repositories shows three chips naming two
projects; a click on a chip of the second project opens that project's cockpit on that job; the
chips read references and titles only, never another project's evidence.

## Risks / anti-goals
No global evidence store: the cockpit reads each project's data through that project's own routes.
