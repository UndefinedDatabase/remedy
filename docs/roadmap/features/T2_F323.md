## Goal & Done
A job waiting for another job of its order never waits forever: when the awaited job ends blocked, stopped, failed, cancelled or declined, the waiting job ends blocked with a readable reason; when the awaited job's result is applied, an unattended waiting job starts.

## What it rests on
- Jobs 43e55dbbd5ba4e73 (waits for 4b90ef2ed0274960), 3d08d387a5374bf9 (waits for e13ea16c239b4d8f) and e94b9c06501c4495 (waits for 78010133a31d43fb) stayed waiting after the awaited job blocked.

## Acceptance
- An awaited job that ends blocked leaves its waiting job blocked with a sentence naming the awaited job and its reason.
- An applied awaited job starts its unattended waiting job.

## Built state (2026-10-29)
- `order_jobs.settle_waiting_jobs`, `awaited_job_end`, `release_waiting_job`, `start_released_job` and the keys `released_job` and `unattended`; `record_job_waits` names the released job on the awaited one.
- Called by `job_run_finish.stamp_blocked_job_finish` (`settle_jobs_waiting_for`) after every `run_job`, also one that raised, by `job_cancel._settle_now`, by the decline in `do_cmd`, and after an HTTP apply in `public_api.answer_public_api_post`. - Tests: `tests/orchestration/test_order_jobs.py` (F323 section). Documented in `docs/system/public-http-api-v1.md` (Orders) and the apply route's description.
