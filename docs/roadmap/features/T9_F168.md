## Design

EVERY chapter header: 'Technical input - not a legal document, no conformity claim.' A --brief SME variant exists.

## Task slicing

## Acceptance & tests
Every statement carries an artifact link (a link checker walks the dossier); the golden is stable (F012); period filters reconcile with the ledger.

## Risks / anti-goals
NO legal terms as assurances ('compliant', 'satisfies Art. X') - only 'provides evidence for' phrasing; reviewing that wording is part of acceptance.
