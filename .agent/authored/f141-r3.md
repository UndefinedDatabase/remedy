   - `WRITE_PERMISSION_REFUSED = "permission_refused"`, the prefix of a refused `write_worktree`.
   - `apply_permission_refusals(job: Any, result: Any) -> list[str]`: the actions are
     `write_worktree`, then `commit` when `result.commit_message_mode` is a key of
     `COMMIT_MODE_FLAGS`, then `push` when `result.push`. The request is
     `PermissionRequest(level=pinned_job_level(str(job.job_id)), subject=f"the apply of job {job.job_id}", job_id=str(job.job_id))`.
     It calls `require_permissions(actions, request)` with no approval. A `PermissionRefusedError`
     answers exactly one refusal, `f"{prefix}: {exc.reason}"`, `prefix` being `PUSH_REFUSED` for
     `push`, `HISTORY_REFUSED` for `commit` in mode `history`, `COMMIT_REFUSED` for `commit` in the
     other two modes, and `WRITE_PERMISSION_REFUSED` for any other action, and writes nothing.
     Otherwise, when `result.status == "approved_apply_started"` (the call right before anything is
     written), it calls `require_permission(action, ...)` once per action in that order, with
     `InstanceApproval(APPROVAL_KIND_FLAG, flag)`, `flag` being `"--approve"`, the commit mode's flag
     from `COMMIT_MODE_FLAGS`, or `"--push"`; then it answers `[]`.
   - `push_permission_refusals(job_id: str | None) -> list[str]`: `require_permission("push", ...)`
     at `pinned_job_level(job_id)` with subject `"the push of a remedy do walk"` and no approval;
     `[exc.reason]` on a `PermissionRefusedError`, else `[]`. Writes nothing.
   - `grant_push_permission(job_id: str | None, approval_ref: str) -> str`: the same request with
     `InstanceApproval(APPROVAL_KIND_FLAG, approval_ref)`; `""` when allowed (an `ask` is audited by
     the check), the refusal's reason on a `PermissionRefusedError`. Never raises it.
