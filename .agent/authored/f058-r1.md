    `REVIEWER_WORKER_CLASS_PAIRS`, with the worker side's strongest tier the maximum, by
    `model_tier_rank`, of the worker class's routed tier and every worker-side link's tier: a
    reviewer-side link is checked with `check_reviewer_not_weaker_than_worker(strongest,
    link.tier)` and a worker-side link with `check_reviewer_not_weaker_than_worker(link.tier,

    role in the table's order, then position, then rule in `FAILOVER_RULE_NAMES`' order.
  - `build_failover_chains(raw, effective_tiers=None) -> dict[...]` — parse, validate, raise
