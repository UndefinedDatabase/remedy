### Q14 — What a mission means now (2026-10-12, F297, round 10)

**What needs deciding.** The page that fixes the meaning of the words Remedy uses still said that
every order you give becomes a mission of its own. Since your change of the eleventh of October,
Remedy keeps one lasting mission for each repository, and every order you give for that
repository is filed in it as one more batch of work. The loop rewrote the page to say exactly
that, and redrew the small diagram that shows how the words fit together, which also appears at
the top of the project's front page, so that it starts at the mission and shows each order as a
batch under it.

**Why it matters.** The page is binding: every command description and every document must use
the words as it defines them. While it carried the old meaning, new command descriptions had to
avoid the word mission altogether to pass the check that reads them.

**My recommendation.** Keep the new wording. It describes what Remedy really does today, and it
lets later descriptions use the word mission again.

**What happens if you say nothing.** The recommendation is already carried out and stands until
you say otherwise.
