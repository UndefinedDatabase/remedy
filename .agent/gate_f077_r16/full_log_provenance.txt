Both raw logs live under .remedy-wt/.cache/gate_r16/, which .gitignore drops
(.gitignore line 235, ".remedy-wt/"). They are deliberately NOT committed: a
log growing inside the tracked tree changes the worktree digest mid-run and
turns the manifest-identity ids red as false positives, and each full log is
far past the 500-insertion commit cap. The committed *_run_tail.txt files carry
the command, the SHA, the exit code, the summary line and the last 40 lines;
